Code of Ethics & Conduct

What every UCC creator stands for

Being part of UCC means holding ourselves to a higher standard. These principles guide every post, live stream, video and comment we make.

01

Truth & Accuracy

Verify before you post. No fake news, edited misinformation or rumors presented as fact. If unsure, say so — or don't post it.

02

Respect & Dignity

No hate speech, bullying, body-shaming, or attacks on anyone's religion, tribe, gender or background. Disagree with ideas, never degrade people.

03

Privacy & Consent

Ask before filming or posting people. Protect minors, victims, patients and the grieving — blur faces and never share private details.

04

Originality & Credit

No reposting or stealing others' content. Always credit the original creator, photographer or source.

05

No Clickbait or Exploitation

Titles and thumbnails must be honest. Never exploit tragedy, poverty or suffering for views or engagement.

06

Transparency

Clearly disclose sponsored, paid or partnered content. Our audience deserves to know when we are being paid.

07

Collaboration over Competition

Support fellow members, share opportunities and settle disagreements privately and respectfully — never through public call-outs.

08

Community First

Use our reach for public service: emergencies, local announcements, causes and helping those in need.

09

Accountability

When we make a mistake, we correct it publicly and quickly, apologize sincerely and learn from it.

10

Represent UCC Well

Online and offline, our actions reflect the group. Act with integrity at events, in comments and in everyday life.

The UCC Pledge

Every member commits to these principles when joining the group.

"I will create with honesty, respect and purpose. I will use my voice to unite, not divide — and to bring positive change to my community."

When the code is broken

  1. A private reminder from the officers.
  2. A formal warning and request to remove or correct the content.
  3. Temporary suspension from UCC activities.
  4. Removal from the group for serious or repeated violations.
